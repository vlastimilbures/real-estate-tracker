# Domain glossary

- **NOI** = effective rent − holding costs.
- **DSCR** = NOI ÷ debt service (>1 = rent covers the mortgage).
- **LTV** = debt ÷ value.
- **Cap rate / net yield** = NOI ÷ value.
- **Gross yield** = gross rent ÷ value.
- **Equity** = value − debt.
- **Nominal** = future CZK; **Real** = today's purchasing power (deflated by CPI).
- **Annuity** = constant payment, interest-heavy early, principal-heavy later.
- **Fixation** = the years a CZ mortgage's rate is fixed; at the end it **resets** to
  `postFixationResetRate` and the instalment re-amortizes the balance over the remaining term.
- **SVJ / fond oprav** = building-repair fund (monthly).
- **daň z nemovitých věcí** = property tax (annual).
- **CAGR** = smoothed annual growth.
- **IRR** = the single annual return that balances the cash-flow vector.

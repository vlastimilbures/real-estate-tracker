# 0005. UX: audit plus quick wins

- Status: Superseded by 0081
- Date: 2026-09-30 (taken before the refactor programme; recorded in P0)
- Source IDs: D-05

## Context

A full UX redesign would dwarf the hardening work; a structured audit with only high-impact, low-effort fixes keeps scope bounded.

## Decision

**UX/UI: audit plus quick wins.** Only high-impact, low-effort fixes are implemented; the rest goes to a backlog.

## Consequences

P7's scope is limited by the quick-win definition (PLAN §10, P6).
